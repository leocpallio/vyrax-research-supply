## 4. Frequently asked questions (12)

**Who can order from Vyrax Labs?**
Orders are accepted from adults aged 18 or over who are buying for legitimate laboratory, analytical
or educational research. Every order includes a confirmation that the materials will not be used in
humans or animals. All products are for in-vitro laboratory research, and are not medicines, foods or
cosmetics.

**Why is payment an enquiry rather than an instant card payment?**
Card payment is being set up, so nothing is charged on this site. When you place an order it reaches
us as an enquiry: we check every line against stock, then send payment details to your email address
within one working day. We never ask for card details by email or message.

**Do you offer multi-buy or volume pricing?**
Orders over £150 receive 10% off, over £250 receive 15% off and over £400 receive 20% off, applied
automatically in the basket. Retatrutide 10mg and Tirzepatide 20mg are also on a buy two, get the
third free offer. Research institutions and trade buyers can contact us about larger volumes.

**How quickly do you dispatch?**
Orders confirmed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. Orders confirmed after 2pm,
at weekends or on bank holidays are dispatched on the next working day. Dispatch is by Royal Mail
Tracked 24.

**How much is delivery, and how is it tracked?**
UK delivery is free on orders over £50; below that a flat fee of £4.99 applies. Every parcel is
tracked, and the tracking number is sent on dispatch. Vials travel padded inside a plain outer parcel
with no product names on the outside.

**What happens if my parcel is lost or arrives damaged?**
Contact us within 14 days of the expected delivery date with your order number. If the carrier
confirms the parcel is lost, or a vial arrives damaged, we reship it free of charge. For damage, a
photograph of the parcel and its contents helps us resolve it quickly.

**How is purity verified right now?**
Batches are submitted to an independent laboratory for HPLC purity analysis and mass spectrometry
identity confirmation. No certificates are published yet: results appear on our Lab Reports page as
each launch batch clears testing, and nothing is listed until we hold it. Until then, that page shows
the testing status of each batch.

**How do I check the certificate for my vial?**
Every vial carries a batch number. Batch lookup on our Lab Reports page opens as certificates are
published, so the number printed on your vial will resolve to its own certificate. Until the first
certificates are live, that page shows each batch status and the lookup says no certificate is
published yet.

**What form are the peptides supplied in, and can they be used in humans?**
Peptides are supplied as a lyophilised (freeze-dried) powder in sealed glass vials. Bacteriostatic
water for reconstitution is sold separately. They are not medicines and must not be used in humans or
animals.

**How should I store lyophilised peptides?**
For long-term storage keep sealed vials frozen at -20°C, away from light and moisture. For shorter
periods, 2 to 8°C refrigeration is suitable. Store it as directed as soon as your parcel arrives. Once
reconstituted, keep the solution refrigerated at 2 to 8°C and avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.

**Can I return an order?**
Unopened items in their original condition can be returned within 14 days of delivery. If a product
has a quality problem, contact us and we will replace it or refund you. We cannot accept opened
vials. See the Returns page for full details.

**Is Vyrax Labs a real company?**
Vyrax Labs is a UK-based supplier of research materials, operating through this website. Company
registration details are being finalised and will be published in the site footer once confirmed. We
would rather show an empty space than invent a registration number, so where a fact is not yet
confirmed, the site says so.
